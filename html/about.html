About us

The story behind Aurora Haven.

Our story

The culinary team at Aurora Haven is extraordinary. Led by our head chef, the kitchen crafts a menu that goes beyond traditional boundaries. Every dish is a symphony of flavors, made with locally sourced, seasonal ingredients for a wide range of tastes.

Our mission

At Aurora Haven our mission goes beyond serving food. We are committed to creating a dining experience that leaves a lasting impression on our guests. Our principles guide every part of the restaurant, from the kitchen to the dining room, because we aim to be more than a restaurant: we aim to be a culinary sanctuary.

About this project

Aurora Haven is a fictional restaurant. This website was created by Mina Jevtić, a web developer from Belgrade, as a portfolio project built with HTML and CSS. The dishes, prices and contact details are examples.
